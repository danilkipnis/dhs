Dhs. Tables III–VII — mental‑state network

Cases of rise of thought in Dhs. as described by the tables (III–VIII) of Essay 5, “Rise of Thought as Phenomenon and System,” of Alexander Piatigorsky's The Buddhist Philosophy of Thought: Essays in Interpretation (archive.org). The primary source, the Dhammasangani (Dhs.) itself, is on archive.org too (archive.org).

Bottom–up: mental states (MS, table‑vi) → dharmas (Δ, many MS → one dharma) → cases (many dharmas → one case, table‑v). Above cases: nomenclature (table‑iii), sphere of thought (table‑iv), variants of content / trancic / mental / object variables (table‑v's dimension tables), each spanning many cases → avacara & the three quality tiers (dharma/thought/action), each spanning many spheres. The green column at right holds table‑vii's per‑case dharma supersets. Case hover text also cites its Dhs. paragraph & page (table‑viii) where transcribed.
